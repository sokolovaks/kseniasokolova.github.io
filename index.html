Hi, I'm Ksenia

I'm a business student at Northeastern's D'Amore-McKim School of Business, based in Boston and graduating in December 2026. My concentrations are Marketing, Marketing Analytics, and Data Science, which basically means I like the creative side of a brand and I also like opening the spreadsheet to see whether it actually worked.

I've done two co-ops that showed me both sides of that. At Brown Brothers Harriman I worked in Firmwide Brand Marketing, and at Lunar Solar Group I was a Search Engine Marketing Junior Analyst. One taught me how a big, established brand tells its story. The other taught me how much you can learn from search data when you pay attention to it.

Outside of class, I'm the President of Northeastern's Culture & Language Learning Society (NUCALLS), where I used to be Treasurer, and I'm an event coordinator for the Fashion Society. I also spent a semester in Paris with the Fashion Dialogue of Civilizations program, which is still one of my favorite things I've done at Northeastern.

This site is a quick tour of who I am and what I've worked on. If you want the longer version, head over to the My Work page, and feel free to reach out using the links at the bottom.
