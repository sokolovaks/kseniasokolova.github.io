Where marketing meets data

I love the combination of creativity and analytics. My marketing classes gave me the language for positioning, storytelling, and audiences. My analytics and data science classes gave me the tools to test whether those ideas hold up. I try to use both whenever I can, because a good idea with no numbers behind it is just an opinion, and a pile of numbers with no story is hard for anyone to care about.

Paris, during the Fashion Dialogue of Civilizations program.

My co-ops are where this got real. In Firmwide Brand Marketing at Brown Brothers Harriman, I got to see how a large financial firm thinks about its brand across many teams. As a Search Engine Marketing Junior Analyst at Lunar Solar Group, I worked with search campaign data and learned how small changes can shift results. My leadership roles at NUCALLS and the Fashion Society pushed me in a different direction: budgeting, planning events, and getting people to show up.
